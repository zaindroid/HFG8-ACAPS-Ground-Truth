No platform exists. Honesty is the point.

Integrity and honesty

We describe bias to the analyst and state our limits in public.

  • ACAPS's rating is hidden until the expert submits (anchoring).
  • Equal-weight mean, with spread and minority reasons.
  • Notes for the analyst: convergence, shared origin, coverage gaps. They describe; they do not judge.
  • Honesty box on every ledger entry: limits, misses, the decision it could inform.

One validation layer among others. Commitments prove timing, not secrecy from the operator. Hardening is built, each item with its limit: trust-design.md.
