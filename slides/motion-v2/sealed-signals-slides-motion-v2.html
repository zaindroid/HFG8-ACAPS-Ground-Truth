The challenge

How can ACAPS turn scattered expert knowledge into sharper risk analysis, without costing experts their time or their anonymity?

Expert knowledge stays in informal chats and disappears. No platform captures it.
The constraints: minutes, not hours. Anonymity under the expert’s control.
The open problem: a panel can anchor, follow the crowd or be steered.
Two directions, one loop: react and forecast.
2 / 9
Our solution

A platform that collects expert judgement with the bias designed out.

Collect
No sign-up. One screen by email, WhatsApp or SMS. A forecast and what is missing, in a minute, rating hidden.
Aggregate
Committed answers open together. The panel view shows the mean, the spread and the minority reasons.
Improve
Resolved risks are scored. Calibration feedback and reliability weights sharpen the panel over time.
The result: better-grounded local information, with its limits stated.
3 / 9
Why it reduces bias

Each known bias gets its own countermeasure.

Anchoring
ACAPS’s rating stays hidden until the expert has submitted their own estimate.
Herding
Answers are committed with a timestamp and opened together.
Social pressure
Each expert picks a mask: known, pseudonym or anonymous. Admins see the masked view.
Overconfidence
Confidence is asked for. Calibration feedback stays private, never a public score.
Knowledge gap
Occasional seed questions show the gap between confidence and knowledge.
Echo chamber
The panel is matched on place and theme, with at most 3 of 8 from one affiliation.
Rumour
A saw, read or heard tag. Claims with a shared origin count once.
Designed to reduce bias, not to prove its absence. The December pilot is the first test with real experts.
Grounding: Tversky and Kahneman, Delphi method, Pennycook and Rand, Tetlock, Cooke (seed questions).

How it works

The machine rings the doorbell, the panel answers, the analyst decides, the ledger remembers.

Alerts are arithmetic on ACAPS monthly data, no model. Nothing changes an ACAPS product unless the analyst accepts.

From answers to accuracy

Aggregation sharpens the picture. Every resolved risk sharpens the panel.

1
Panel view
The equal-weight mean is the headline, with the spread and the minority reasons next to it.
2
Analyst decides
A cited draft. Accept, edit or reject. Only the analyst changes a rating.
3
Score
When the risk resolves, each forecast is scored against the outcome (Brier), committee-side.
4
Learn
Weights start equal and are capped. Experts hear the group result within a day.
Public honesty box: what fed in, the limits, the misses.
Surprisingly-popular method: experimental, labelled.
