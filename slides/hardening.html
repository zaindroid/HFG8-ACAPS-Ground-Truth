Appendix A. Hardening: what each piece guarantees

Six mechanisms, each with its limit.

Registry encryption (ML-KEM-768)Long-horizon confidentiality of the identity map.Not against an admin who holds the key.
Signed outcomes (ML-DSA-65)Outcome record issued by the committee key, unaltered.Not the truth of the outcome.
One-time credentialsA vetted member answered; ACAPS cannot link it to the approval.Not timing, network details or wording.
Panel membership proofA member answered, no double answers.Off unless stable.
Daily timestamp anchorCommitments existed by that time.Nothing else. Pending until confirmed.
Registry access logACAPS access to identities is visible.Does not prevent an authorised read.

Plus descriptive pattern reports: committee-side, opt-in for the expert, never a score. Demo build: reference libraries, demo keys. Source: trust-design.md, privacy-engineering.md.

Appendix B. What we did not do, and why

The numbers raise the alert, people make the call.

  • No public scores or leaderboards. Scores belong to forecasts and stay with the committee.
  • No automatic decisions. Only an analyst's accept, edit or reject changes a rating.
  • No blockchain logic or tokens. One daily timestamp, nothing else.
  • No claim of secrecy from the operator. Commitments prove timing and consistency.
  • No model in the alerts. Arithmetic on ACAPS monthly data.
