## Must-haves, said in the jury's words

- Easy to use: one screen, one tap, no account, any channel. Show the card.
- Expert-controlled anonymity: the mask is chosen per answer (named, nickname, no identity kept). Show the picker.
- Public anonymity: the public ledger shows aggregates of five or more and never an individual. Show the ledger.
In Q&A, if asked whether answers are anonymous from ACAPS: "pseudonymised to ACAPS, with every access logged; anonymous
to peers and the public. We say exactly that in the product."

## Q&A, two minutes: the eight likely questions and the answers

1. Is it anonymous? Pseudonymised to ACAPS with logged access; anonymous to peers and the public; the expert chooses per answer.
2. Why would an expert answer? No sign-up, one tap, in their own channel; they get the outcome report; they are never ranked.
3. What if the panel is packed or biased? Committee vetting, affiliation cap, coverage-gap note; patterns described, nothing automatic.
4. Does the AI decide anything? No. It drafts with citations; a checker rejects unsupported numbers; the analyst decides and the decision is on record.
5. Is this a blockchain? No. A timestamped commitment and an independent timestamp; we say what each proves.
6. Where does the alert come from? A deterministic diff of ACAPS's own monthly editions; April 2026 Chad reproduced live.
7. What is real and what is synthetic? Live ACAPS API with cache; synthetic experts, labelled; the sensitivity test is a software test.
8. Business model? Inside ACAPS's funded analysis first; then a hosted service per organisation, open-source core.
