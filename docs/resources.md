## Forecasting and Expert Elicitation

These are the methods and platforms that inspired the challenge. Study them for ideas on expert engagement, scoring, and community building.

| Resource | Description | Link |
|----------|-------------|------|
| Early Warning Project | Crowd forecasting with an expert opinion pool, by the US Holocaust Memorial Museum | [earlywarningproject.ushmm.org](https://earlywarningproject.ushmm.org/) |
| The Delphi Method | Building expert consensus in structured rounds — the classic approach to expert elicitation | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delphi_method) |
| Good Judgment Project | The "superforecasters" — decades of research on who predicts well and why | [goodjudgment.com](https://goodjudgment.com/) |
| Metaculus | Community forecasting platform with transparent scoring and track records | [metaculus.com](https://www.metaculus.com/) |
| INFER | Forecasting platform backed by RAND, focused on geopolitical and technology questions | [infer-pub.com](https://www.infer-pub.com/) |

## Technical Inspiration

Ideas for implementation approaches — not requirements, just starting points:

- **Delphi-style surveys:** Experts respond independently, see anonymised group summaries, refine in rounds
- **Prediction markets / scoring rules:** Brier scores, log scoring for calibration tracking
- **Structured analytic techniques:** Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH), key assumptions checks
- **Community design patterns:** Reputation systems, notification design, progressive engagement
