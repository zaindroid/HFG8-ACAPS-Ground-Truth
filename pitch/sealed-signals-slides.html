The problem: risk ratings are uncertain, and the expert checks that should sharpen them leave nothing behind.
47 %

Medium-rated risks are a coin flip

27 of 58 resolved Medium risks materialised; 32 of 38 High. The rating is where preparedness money starts.

Expert checks leave no trail

Between scheduled reviews, analysts check with experts by email and conversation. No structure, no timestamp, no record of who said what before the outcome. Experts will not sign up to a portal to change that.

?

Nobody can see the bias or the misses

Anchoring on the published rating, herding, loud majorities, packed panels. No feedback to the expert, no public record of what fed into a rating or when it was wrong.

Numbers from the ACAPS Risk Radar export, 139 risks, 104 resolved; the mentor's own words: "we will get things wrong; the point is honesty about it"1
The solution: a one-minute expert loop on top of ACAPS's data, with bias designed out, every claim checked, and a public record that shows its misses.

Model-optional by design

Deterministic template is the default path; the same Skeptic and Judge checks run on it. The LLM loop sits behind FF_AGENTS and is never expert-facing.

Verification queue

Uncorroborated claims wait for the analyst with their source tag. Access to the queue is logged, because that is where distinctive text lives.

Hard fails

Aggregate with n under 5. A number off by any amount. A claim without a record. Each blocks the draft until repaired.

Audit

Skeptic verdicts and Judge scores are stored next to the decision on the ledger.

Commit and reveal

Proves answers existed at that time and never changed; blocks peer anchoring. Not secrecy from the operator.

Masks, HMAC pseudonyms

Proves confidentiality from peers and public, with logged ACAPS access. Not anonymity from ACAPS; we say pseudonymised.

Blind-signed credentials, RFC 9474

Proves a vetted member answered, unlinkable to the approval; reuse rejected. Not timing correlation.

Zero-knowledge membership, Semaphore v4

Proves "a member of this panel answered once". Built, flag off until stable.

Public timestamp, Sigstore Rekor

Proves independently that the daily root existed by then. Only the fingerprint is logged; no answers, no names, no cost.

Post-quantum: ML-KEM-768, ML-DSA-65

Proves long-horizon confidentiality of the identity map; outcome records issued by the committee key. Not secrecy from an admin with the key.

Experts

Mask per answer; pseudonymised to peers, limits stated. Committed before anyone sees it, revealed together, so no one anchors on them. Never scored as a person; calibration only with consent, and they see it first. Outcome report for every answer.

Analysts

Alerts from arithmetic on ACAPS's own editions. Bias patterns described, never verdicts. Every number in the draft checked against a versioned record; uncorroborated claims held in a queue. Their decision and rationale on record.

ACAPS committee

Vetted panels, two approvals, affiliation cap. Identity map encrypted with post-quantum key establishment; every read logged. One-time blind-signed credentials prove a vetted member answered without linking the answer to the approval. Outcome records signed with ML-DSA-65.

Readers and donors

A public ledger with the group view, the decision it could inform, the limits, and the misses, including when the analyst overruled a panel that was right. One click recomputes the commitment hash. Aggregates only at five or more; never an individual.

What we do not claim. Commitments prove timing and consistency, not secrecy from the operator. Masks give confidentiality from peers and the public, not anonymity from ACAPS. Anchoring is a timestamp, not a blockchain product. Scores are for forecasts, never for people.
