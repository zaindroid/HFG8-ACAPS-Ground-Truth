WhoBefore the revealAfterEver
The experttheir own answerthe group view, ACAPS's band, the decisiontheir calibration, if they ask, and they see it first
Peersnothingthe spread, unattributed disagreementnever a name
ACAPSthat an answer existsanswers under the mask chosenthe registry, with every read logged
The publicnothingaggregates of five or more, the honesty boxnever an individual

Pseudonymised, not anonymous: we say which. Commitments prove timing, not secrecy from the operator. Scores are for forecasts, panel-level; nothing is measured about a person without consent.

Built and tested behind flags, described on the repo page with what each proves: post-quantum key establishment for the registry, signed outcome attestations, one-time blind-signed credentials, timestamp anchoring.
